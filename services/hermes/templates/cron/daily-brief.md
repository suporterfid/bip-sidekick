# Bip daily brief

Read `/vault/STATUS.md`, `/vault/BACKLOG.md`, recent `/vault/daily/` notes, today's
calendar events, unread or flagged mail, and WhatsApp context when the MCP server is
available.

Scan bill-related Gmail for invoice, due-date, payment confirmation, failed payment, and
overdue signals. Surface upcoming and overdue bills when they are clear enough to help,
deduplicate repeated notices for the same vendor/account/amount/due date, and cite the
source message when confidence is low or the item is risky.

Choose exactly one next action for today. Explain why in two or three concise sentences.

Write the brief to `/vault/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and send the same brief to Telegram.

Do not send email, send WhatsApp messages, deploy, spend money, delete data, or mutate
external systems. Do not click payment links, pay bills, mark mail read, delete mail, or
contact vendors. If a useful next step requires action, describe the proposed action and stop
for manual approval.
